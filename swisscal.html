SwissCal is a pocket utility for money, health, and time.

It brings together bill splitting, price comparison, discount checks, VAT or GST, BMI, WHtR, exchange rates, and a haptic timer in one Flutter app.

03 / Core tools

A calculator for the moment.

The app is designed around situations rather than formulas, so people can move from a real-life need to a useful result with less mental work.

01↗

Bill split

Divides a shared bill between people and helps account for service charges, so a group can settle up without doing the arithmetic by hand.

02⇄

Compare price

Compares the unit price of different pack sizes. It makes the better-value choice clear when products are not sold in equal quantities.

03%

Discount

Calculates the sale price and savings from a percentage discount, making promotions easier to check before buying.

04+

VAT / GST

Adds or removes tax from an amount so the pre-tax price, tax amount, and final total are easy to understand.

05BMI

BMI

Uses height and weight to estimate body mass index, offering a quick, general reference point for a person’s weight range.

06WH

WHtR

Calculates waist-to-height ratio, a simple screening measure that puts waist size in the context of a person’s height.

07¥

Exchange rate

Converts between currencies using a clear amount-and-rate layout—handy for travel, online shopping, or quick cost estimates.

08◷

Haptic timer

Runs a focused timer with tactile feedback, allowing users to keep track of an interval without constantly looking at the screen.
